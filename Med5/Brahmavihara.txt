Ahaṁ sukhito homi. Niddukkho homi. Avero homi. Abyāpajjho homi. Anīgho homi. Sukhī attānaṁ pariharāmi
Sabbe sattā sukhitā hontu.
=== The necessity of Metta for spiritual development ===
+ Dosa is bondage to the mind. Viz fire burn thru forest.
   Metta (thru stillness & seclusion) cleanse dosa, broken barriers. Viz as seed, sapling.
+ Dosa grow easily, by objectification/ dehumanization/ cruelty. Disguise as justice, avoidance.
   Metta is difficult to grow, by directed toward unlimited beings (not concepts). Disguise by attachment.
   Metta for general - all, Karuna for one who suffered, Mudita for one who happy, Upekkha for keep being real.
+ Dosa root in asava: tan.haa & moha.
   Metta root in effort, purity (concept of beauty) & divine abode, which go against the kaama loka 
+ Avoidance & Delusion as the smoke.

=== Self: May I be happy > Make myself as example/kindle. I want to be happy and not pain, so too are others. ===
+ **Kaya**: Chest-Anap-Aliveness: May I not riddled with sickness & pain. May I'll be healthy and aged with dignity. 
+ **Vedana**: Heart: May I not lost heart against the flow of the world. May I not be stucked & become a sad, lonely, angry, bitter & cynical old man. May I be free.
+ **Sanna**: brain center: May I not poisoned/corrupted by 5 nīvaraṇa (include loneliness, depression). May I gain foothold in samadhi
+ **Sankara**: bindi: May I not live in undecision, lazy & regret. May I be resolve with certainty base on truth
+ **Vinnana**: tophead: May I not be bounded. May I be free.
=== **Others: connection as amplifier for Metta, remove hostile & indifference, breaking down barriers** ===
+ Social Circle: Kin, Friends, Colleague, Accquantance, Collegue: Gratitude to who make this life possible.
+ Human World: lonely old people, orphanage, homeless, disable.
+ Animal World & Ecosystem: Mamals, Bird, Reptile, Insect,... Social, Social Near Extincted, Solitary. 
+ Invisible World: Deva, hungry ghost, Hell.
=== Self & Other: ===
In samsara, everyone has once been family, and you once love so much/ endure them.  
=== Citta-Infinity: divine abode: unbound by limitation (definition, space-time, mind-realm,...) ===
+ Space: like space, without limit, without obstruction.
+ Time: maintain in every posture: sit, standup, walk or lie down.
+ Reality: Divine abode: spring joy & peace from inside. Init spread it with effort, but then just realize it part of your reality (consciousness).
+ Mind: Metta is protected, respected, nurtured like my newborn baby.

=== Sattabojjhaṅgā - Subha-paramāhaṁ ([[https://suttacentral.net/sn46.54/en/sujato|sn46.54]]) ===
Sace ākaṅkhati ‘appaṭikūle paṭikūlasaññī vihareyyan’ti, paṭikūlasaññī tattha viharati.
Sace ākaṅkhati ‘paṭikūle appaṭikūlasaññī vihareyyan’ti, appaṭikūlasaññī tattha viharati.
Sace ākaṅkhati ‘appaṭikūle ca paṭikūle ca paṭikūlasaññī vihareyyan’ti, paṭikūlasaññī tattha viharati.
Sace ākaṅkhati ‘paṭikūle ca appaṭikūle ca appaṭikūlasaññī vihareyyan’ti, appaṭikūlasaññī tattha viharati.
Sace ākaṅkhati ‘appaṭikūlañca paṭikūlañca tadubhayaṁ abhinivajjetvā upekkhako vihareyyaṁ sato sampajāno’ti, upekkhako ca tattha viharati sato sampajāno, subhaṁ vā kho pana vimokkhaṁ upasampajja viharati.
Subha-paramāhaṁ, **mettācetovimuttiṁ** vadāmi, idhapaññassa bhikkhuno uttarivimuttiṁ appaṭivijjhato.
Sabbaso vā pana rūpasaññānaṁ samatikkamā paṭighasaññānaṁ atthaṅgamā nānattasaññānaṁ amanasikārā ‘ananto ākāso’ti ākāsānañcāyatanaṁ upasampajja viharati.
Ākāsānañcāyatanaparamāhaṁ, **karuṇācetovimuttiṁ** vadāmi, idhapaññassa bhikkhuno uttarivimuttiṁ appaṭivijjhato.
Sabbaso vā pana ākāsānañcāyatanaṁ samatikkamma ‘anantaṁ viññāṇan’ti viññāṇañcāyatanaṁ upasampajja viharati.
Viññāṇañcāyatanaparamāhaṁ, **muditācetovimuttiṁ** vadāmi, idhapaññassa bhikkhuno uttarivimuttiṁ appaṭivijjhato.
Sabbaso vā pana viññāṇañcāyatanaṁ samatikkamma ‘natthi kiñcī’ti ākiñcaññāyatanaṁ upasampajja viharati.
Ākiñcaññāyatanaparamāhaṁ, **upekkhācetovimuttiṁ** vadāmi, idhapaññassa bhikkhuno uttarivimuttiṁ appaṭivijjhato”ti.
