**Eye**: opaque, white by rotting, eaten by microorg: continuous degradation inherent in body, being-nature-core-existence.

**Brain**: liquidfied: self instance (in continuous existence of samsara) is just a strategy to search for food, not against M.

**Nose**: rot out: the stopping of Anap could happen at anytime, anywhere > Value of time, even space between Anap.

**Face**: The social identity mean nothing.
