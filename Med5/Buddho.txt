Namo tassa bhagavato arahato samasambuddhassa | Namo Buddhaya | Buddham saraṇam gacchami
Itipi so bhagavā: arahaṁ sammāsambuddho vijjācaraṇasampanno sugato lokavidū anuttaro purisadammasārathi satthā devamanussānaṁ buddho bhagavā’ti. Yo imaṁ lokaṁ sadevakaṁ samārakaṁ sabrahmakaṁ sassamaṇabrāhmaṇiṁ pajaṁ sadevamanussaṁ sayaṁ abhiññā sacchikatvā pavedesi. Yo dhammaṁ desesi ādikalyāṇaṁ majjhekalyāṇaṁ pariyosānakalyāṇaṁ, sātthaṁ sabyañjanaṁ, kevalaparipuṇṇaṁ parisuddhaṁ brahmacariyaṁ pakāsesi. Tam-ahaṁ bhagavantaṁ abhipūjayāmi, Tam-ahaṁ bhagavantaṁ sirasā namāmi.
=== Bodhi-satto, Arahato, Sugato**:** ===
bodhi-satta: one who is devoted/ addicted/ attached/ fixed to bodhi. [[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bodhisattva#Etymology|Etymology]]
**Viz**: Buddhapada, [[https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1_V5DUQ7Wwk4EZdQfVDJJIzx3Tbwhetlz|Pic_Ascetic]]
**Pamāda-mada**: Blinded Eye: [[https://suttacentral.net/an3.39/en/sujato|an3.39 Sukhumālasutta]] + [[https://suttacentral.net/mn130/en/sujato|MN130 Devadūtasutta]]
**Pariyesanā**: resolved in the Search: Even death or nothing. [[https://suttacentral.net/mn26/en/sujato|MN 26]] - [[https://suttacentral.net/snp3.1/en/sujato|snp3.1 Pabbajjāsutta]] 
+ Pamādo maccuno padaṁ; Appamattā na mīyanti, ye pamattā yathā matā. (Dhammapada Appamāda)
+ Dvemā pariyesanā : anariyā ca ariyā. Sambādho gharāvāso rajāpatho, abbhokāso pabbajjā. 
+ This life at home is cramped, a realm of dirt. The life of one gone forth is like an open space. 
+ [[https://suttacentral.net/dn2/en/sujato?lang=en|DN2]]
**Tapassī**-cīvara: [[https://suttacentral.net/mn4/en/sujato|MN 04-]][[https://suttacentral.net/mn12/en/sujato|MN12-]][[https://suttacentral.net/mn36/en/sujato|MN36]][[https://suttacentral.net/mn12/en/sujato|:]] for purify of being.
+ Cloth is harsh & rough: Kusacīra: made from human hair, horse tail, bird feathers, tree bark, hemp, dry grass.
+ Cloth tattered, smudged (by blood, putrefaction liquid,..): Chavadussa: pick up from trash or from corpse.
+ Skin white by dirt: Rajojallaṁ: No bath & let dirt build up on body for several years.
+ Hair unkempt: Kesamassulocakopi: Pull out hair & beard by hand.
**Tapassī**-āhāra: belly's skin stuck to backbone, eye sunk deep & no strength to stand up > The less he eat, the more clear mind become.
+ Material: From forest: No meat, forest roots and fruits, fallen fruit, herb, wild rice, grass.
+ Material: From village: mung beans, sesame, raw rice, rice bran, boiling rice's scum, cow dung, even his own urine and excrement
+ Amount: Ekālopiko: eat a cup once per day,..per week.
**Tapassī**-senāsana: Midnight in 
+ Seclused haunted forest: araññāyatanaṁ....bhiṁsanakaṁ vanasaṇḍaṁ :  In freezing January cold, stayed in the open by night and in the forest by day. In hot summer day, stay in the open by day and in the forest by night. Avoiding people like wild animals.
+ Charnel ground with equinimity: susāne...gāmaṇḍalā: Sleep with the bones of the dead for a pillow, surrounded by harmful people who piss to him & pock stick to his ear.
+ Creepy forests & abandoned shrines:[[https://suttacentral.net/mn4/en/sujato|:]] ārāmacetiyāni vanacetiyāni rukkhacetiyāni. pitch-back when night fall with strange noise, not tolerate fear & dread.
**Tapassī**-paṭipada: The less extreme version (Dhutanga posture,..) still used as it is suitable.
+ Sila: Sati over Dukkha/Sukkha: [[https://suttacentral.net/mn36/en/sujato|MN36:]] Vedanā cittaṁ na pariyādāya tiṭṭhati: Dukkha/Sukkha
+ Posture: ubbhaṭṭhakopi.ukkuṭikopi.kaṇṭakāpassayikopi: Only stand, no sit, no lie down. 
+ Samadhi: Crushed mind with mind until sweat ran from armpits. Breathless absorption even when ear screeching, headache, sharp pain in belly or body burning.
**Adhiṭṭhāna**: Moksha just simply larger than life: 
+ Māra the Wicked is always waiting nearby, thinking: Hopefully I can find a vulnerability. [[https://suttacentral.net/sn35.240/en/sujato|sn35.240 Kummopamasutta]]
+ Let my blood dry, and muscle wasted away...the mind grows clearer...Do I carry muñja grass? I spit on my life. Death in battle'd be better than I, defeated, survive. [[https://suttacentral.net/snp3.2/en/sujato|snp3.2 Padhānasutta/]] [[https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/kn/snp/snp.3.02.than.html|ver 2]]
+ Kāmaṁ taco ca nhāru ca aṭṭhi ca avasissatu, sarīre upassussatu maṁsa lohitaṁ, yaṁ taṁ purisa-thāmena purisa-vīriyena purisa-parakkamena pattabbaṁ na taṁ apāpuṇitvā vīriyassa saṇṭhānaṁ bhavissatī. [[https://suttacentral.net/an2.1-10/en/sujato|AN 2.5]]
+ Aho vatāhaṁ tadantaraṁ jīveyyaṁ yadantaraṁ assasitvā vā passasāmi, passasitvā vā assasāmi, bhagavato sāsanaṁ manasi kareyyaṁ, bahu vata me kataṁ assā. [[https://suttacentral.net/an6.19/en/sujato|AN6.19]]

=== Buddho, Lokavido, vijjācaraṇasampanno ===
**Viz**: Bodhi Leaf, Sunrise + Bodhi Tree + River
**Crossleg & Straight but relax back**: Middle way: [[https://suttacentral.net/sn56.11/en/sujato|sn56.11]]
**Eye** **haft close & Smile**: Enjoy Nibbana: Dhammapada House builder + [[https://suttacentral.net/ud1.1/en/sujato?lang=en|Ud 1.1]]
**Dawn-Sun & Bodhi Tree**: 4 ariya sacca & 12 duties: Ye ca atītā sambuddhā, ye ca buddhā anāgatā; Yo cetarahi sambuddho, bahūnaṁ sokanāsano. Sabbe saddhammagaruno, vihaṁsu viharanti ca; Tathāpi viharissanti, esā buddhāna dhammatā. [[https://suttacentral.net/sn6.2/en/sujato?lang=en|SN 6.2]]
**Halo-Radiance-Pabhāva**: **Patipadam yathābhūtaṁ pajānāti:** Power & Understand practice as already accomplished.
- Jhāna-vimokkha-samādhi-samāpattīnaṁ saṅkilesaṁ vodānaṁ vuṭṭhānaṁ: [[https://suttacentral.net/mn12/en/sujato|MN12]]: corruption, cleansing, and emergence regarding the absorptions, liberations, immersions, and attainments.
- Sīla, Nīvaraṇappahāna, jhāna, Aṭṭhañāṇa:[[https://suttacentral.net/dn2/en/sujato|DN2:]] Vipassanāñāṇa, Manomayiddhiñāṇa, Iddhividhañāṇa, Dibbasotañāṇa, Cetopariyañāṇa, Pubbenivāsānussatiñāṇa, Dibbacakkhuñāṇa, Āsavakkhayañāṇa
- Sattānaṁ nāna-adhimuttikataṁ, para-sattānaṁ para-puggalānaṁ indriya-paropariyattaṁ, sabbattha-gāminiṁ paṭipadaṁ: [[https://suttacentral.net/mn12/en/sujato|MN12]]: diverse convictions, faculties, practices & their results of beings
- Vesārajja-aṭṭha parisā: [[https://suttacentral.net/mn12/en/sujato|MN12:]] 4 Confident questions & 8 assemblies.

=== Satthā devamanussānaṁ, Sammāsambuddho, anuttaro purisadammasārathi ===
**Viz**: Lotus pond, pagoda with forest.
**Bodhi tree Shade &** **meditation forest**: **Dhammanudhamma Patipada:** Ariya Sanga, complete training in Vipassana
+ Lotus seat: Out of metta, teach the Dhamma that hard to teach, hard to see, hard to understand.
   Rather be alone than in company of fools. [[https://suttacentral.net/dhp60-75/en/sujato|dhp60:]] nādhigaccheyya, Seyyaṁ sadisamattano; Ekacariyaṁ daḷhaṁ kayirā, Natthi bāle sahāyatā. 
+ Dhammacharkra: Dhamma wheel has been turn.
+ Opened Hand: The full teaching is here, without secrets. [[https://suttacentral.net/dn16/en/sujato|DN 16]] Natthānanda, tathāgatassa dhammesu ācariyamuṭṭhi.
+ Do not regret not pratice in future. Out of compassion, what needed the Buddha to be done, has been done, so meditate, pratice. Don’t be negligent! Don’t regret it later! [[https://suttacentral.net/sn47.10/en/sujato|SN 47.10:]] Yaṁ, ānanda, satthārā karaṇīyaṁ...Ayaṁ vo amhākaṁ anusāsanī”ti: 
**The Inner Park:** Accomplish in Panna or Samadhi.
**The Outer Park:** mistake in training, not accomplish in Sila & Samadhi 
+ Saddha in Buddha-Dhamma: deluded by asava like a trapped quail: play pick & chose with training rules, Dhamma... [[https://suttacentral.net/mn66/en/sujato|MN66]]
+ Viriya: not put forth effort in the duties of Learning, Sila, Samadhi & Panna - i.e 8 fold path. 
+ Sati: not see danger in slighest fault in Sila, in Satipatthana.
+ Samadhi: Abandon duties of Samadhi: Not found joy & nutrition in the Dh. [[https://suttacentral.net/mn70/en/sujato|MN 70]] Saddhassa, sāvakassa
+ Panna: Misinterpret Dh base on asava/desire. Consider what's harmful is not. [[https://suttacentral.net/mn22/en/sujato|MN22]] bhikkhave dhammānaṁ.
**Colorful** **busy street outside the gate**: Apārutā tesaṁ amatassa dvārā, Ye sotavanto pamuñcantu saddhaṁ; Adhigato kho myāyaṁ dhammo gambhīro duddaso duranubodho ...nirodho nibbānaṁ: [[https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-kd1/en/brahmali|KD1.]] Anamataggoyaṁ saṁsāro: [[https://suttacentral.net/sn15.1/en/sujato|SN15.1]]: The deathless door opened, small is the number of those entered. Most don't want to hear the Dhamma that is hard to see, enjoying transmigration-samsara what has experienced with blood & tears, not knowing "This is enough to be disillusioned, to be freed".

=== Bhagavāto, Tathāgato, cakkhuṁ loke (eye of the world) [[https://suttacentral.net/dn16/en/sujato|DN 16:]] ===
**Viz:** Reclining Buddha surrounded by Sangha, Ashoka pillars, Stupa -chaitya
+ Buddha's smile: The **work** has been done, out of compassion for beings.
	Eye: Recognition of homage: paṭhameneva yāmena kosinārake malle bhagavantaṁ vandāpesi.
+ Voice: **Last word**: “Handa dāni, bhikkhave, āmantayāmi vo, vayadhammā saṅkhārā, appamādena sampādetha." Ayaṁ tathāgatassa pacchimā vācā.
+ Stilling Anap: **Full Nibbana**: Out of samsara: Atha kho bhagavā paṭhamaṁ jhānaṁ samāpajji,...,“aniccā saṅkhārā, taṁ kutettha labbhā”ti.
+ Song\Cry of the deva: **Heritage-Legacy-Heir in Dhamma**: Etarahi kho pana, bhikkhū-bhikkhuniyo-upāsakā-upāsikā ...suppakāsitaṁ.
	‘aniccā saṅkhārā, taṁ kutettha labbhā’
**Ref**: [[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tath%C4%81gata|Tathāgata,]] [[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parinirvana|Parinirvana]]
