Cūḷasuññatasutta: [sujato](https://suttacentral.net/mn121/en/sujato), [thanissaro](https://www.dhammatalks.org/suttas/MN/MN121.html)
- Recog abscence of stressful subjects & their diversity : suññamidaṁ saññāgataṁ gāmasaññāyā, suññamidaṁ saññāgataṁ manussasaññāyā
- Recog existance of less stress singleness base on subject: bhikkhusaṅghaṁ paṭicca ekattaṁ, araññasaññaṁ paṭicca ekattan
- Regard as empty of what is not there, but as to what remains they understand that it is present.
