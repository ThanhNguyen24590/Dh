=== Bāhirā (external): far/near, past/future/present, blatant/subtle, repulsive/sublime ===
Pathavī-kasiṇam-eko sañjānāti uddhaṁ adho tiriyaṁ advayaṁ appamāṇaṁ; (an10.29)
+ Pathavī: Rock < Building, Places < Moutains < Earth : Deep Time
+ āpo: Raindrop < Puddle < Lake < River < Ocean : Deep Time
+ Vāyo: Breaht < Wind < Winds in field, tall buiding < Cloud < Storm < Sky : Deep Time
+ Tejo: Candle< Camp Fire < Buidling fire < Forest Fire < ... < Sun : Deep Time
+ ākāsa: Cup < House < Sky < Space : Deep Space.
#### Samadhi
+ Yā ceva kho pana ajjhattikā dhātu yā ca bāhirā dhātu dhāturevesā. (Only Dhatu, no separations nor distinction)
+ Pathavīsamaṁ bhāvanaṁ bhāvehi. Pathavīsamañhi te bhāvanaṁ bhāvayato uppannā manāpāmanāpā (agreeable/disagreeable) phassā cittaṁ na pariyādāya (overwhelm) ṭhassanti (remain). Seyyathāpi pathaviyā (clean) nikkhipanti (throw), asucimpi nikkhipanti, gūthagatampi (feces) nikkhipanti, muttagatampi (urine) nikkhipanti, kheḷagatampi (spits) nikkhipanti, pubbagatampi (pus) nikkhipanti, lohitagatampi (blood) nikkhipanti, na ca tena pathavī aṭṭīyati (distressed) vā harāyati (ashamed) vā jigucchati (disgusted) vā; Ākāsasamaṁ, bhāvanaṁ bhāvehi.
+ Āposamaṁ bhāvanaṁ bhāvehi. Āposamañhi te bhāvanaṁ bhāvayato uppannā manāpāmanāpā phassā cittaṁ na pariyādāya ṭhassanti. Seyyathāpi āpasmiṁ sucimpi dhovanti (wash) , asucimpi dhovanti, gūthagatampi dhovanti, muttagatampi dhovanti, kheḷagatampi dhovanti, pubbagatampi dhovanti, lohitagatampi dhovanti, na ca tena āpo aṭṭīyati vā harāyati vā jigucchati vā;
+ Tejosamaṁ bhāvanaṁ bhāvehi. Tejosamañhi te bhāvanaṁ bhāvayato uppannā manāpāmanāpā phassā cittaṁ na pariyādāya ṭhassanti. Seyyathāpi tejo sucimpi dahati (burn), asucimpi dahati, gūthagatampi dahati, muttagatampi dahati, kheḷagatampi dahati, pubbagatampi dahati, lohitagatampi dahati, na ca tena tejo aṭṭīyati vā harāyati vā jigucchati vā;
+ Vāyosamaṁ bhāvanaṁ bhāvehi. Vāyosamañhi te bhāvanaṁ bhāvayato uppannā manāpāmanāpā phassā cittaṁ na pariyādāya ṭhassanti. Seyyathāpi vāyo sucimpi upavāyati (blow), asucimpi upavāyati, gūthagatampi upavāyati, muttagatampi upavāyati, kheḷagatampi upavāyati, pubbagatampi upavāyati, lohitagatampi upavāyati, na ca tena vāyo aṭṭīyati vā harāyati vā jigucchati vā;
+ Ākāsasamaṁ bhāvanaṁ bhāvehi. Ākāsasamañhi te bhāvanaṁ bhāvayato uppannā manāpāmanāpā phassā cittaṁ na pariyādāya ṭhassanti. Seyyathāpi, ākāso na (no) katthaci (somewhere) patiṭṭhito (established);
#### Panna
+ ‘Taṁ netaṁ mama, nesohamasmi, na meso attā’ti (This is not mine, This is not I, This is not myself) evametaṁ yathābhūtaṁ (in trutth) sammappaññāya daṭṭhabbaṁ (to be seen). Evametaṁ yathābhūtaṁ sammappaññāya disvā dhātuyā nibbindati (disenchanted/fed up), dhātuyā cittaṁ virājeti (discard, removed).
+ Tassā hi nāma, bāhirāya pathavīdhātuyā, āpodhātuyā, tejodhātuyā, vāyodhātuyā tāva mahallikāya (great body) aniccatā paññāyissati (revealed to be), khayadhammatā (subject to destruction) paññāyissati, vayadhammatā (subject to disappearance) paññāyissati, vipariṇāmadhammatā (subject to alteration) paññāyissati. Kiṁ pan-imassa matta-ṭṭhakassa (in mere moments) kāyassa taṇh-upādinnassa (taṇhā : cling +upādinna : grasped) ‘ahanti vā mamanti vā asmī’ti vā (take it to be I, mine, or I am) ? Atha khvāssa nocevettha hoti. (One should not consider thus). So phasso aniccoti passati, vedanā aniccāti passati, saññā aniccāti passati, saṅkhārā aniccāti passati, viññāṇaṁ aniccanti passati.Tassa dhāt-ārammaṇam (element) eva (only) cittaṁ pakkhandati (energize) pasīdati (bright) santiṭṭhati (settled) adhimuccati (convinced -confident).
