=== Rupa ===
**Kaya**:
+ Work to make a living: Day job, other people bad day,...
+ Work to maintain body healthy: The bad state of hunger, thirst, pain.
+ Byādhidhammā jarādhammā,...Brahmacariyaparāyaṇo”ti.  [[https://suttacentral.net/an3.39/en/sujato|AN 3.39]]
--------------------
**Kāmā**: aṭṭhikaṅkala (bone)-maṁsapesi-tiṇukkā (grass torch)-aṅgārakāsu (charcoal pit)-supinaka (dream)-yācitaka (borrowed goods)-rukkhaphala-upamā kāmā vuttā bahudukkhā bahupāyāsā, ādīnavo ettha bhiyyo. Evametaṁ yathābhūtaṁ (in truth) sammappaññāya disvā (see) yāyaṁ upekkhā nānattā nānatta (diversity)-sitā (depend on) taṁ abhinivajjetvā (reject), yāyaṁ upekkhā ekattā (unity) ekatta-sitā yattha (where) sabbaso lokāmis-ūpādānā aparisesā (not remain) nirujjhanti (ceases) tamev-ūpekkhaṁ bhāveti. (MN54)
+ Atītampi-anāgatampi-etarahipi paccuppannaṁ addhānaṁ (time) kāmā dukkhasamphassā ceva mahābhitāpā (hot) ca mahāpariḷāhā (burn) ca. Kuṭṭhī (hủi) puriso aru-gatto (aru: sore, pakka: decay, patta: body) pakka-gatto kimīhi (maggot) khajjamāno (eaten) nakhehi (fingernail) vaṇa (wound)-mukhāni (opening) vippa-taccha-māno (scratch open) upahata (injured)-indriyo dukkhasamphasseyeva aggismiṁ sukhamiti viparīta (warped)-saññaṁ paccalatthā (pati+labha: become). Ime ca sattā kāmesu avītarāgā (not-no-raga) kāmataṇhāhi khajjamānā kāma-pariḷāhena (burned) pari-ḍayhamānā (all consumed) upahatindriyā dukkhasamphassesuyeva kāmesu sukhamiti viparītasaññaṁ paccalatthuṁ (MN75).
+ Dīgha-rattaṁ vata bho ahaṁ iminā cittena nikato (deceived) vañcito (tricked) paluddho (seduced). Ahañhi rūpaṁ-vedanaṁ-saññaṁ-saṅkhāraṁ-viññāṇaṁ yeva upādiyamāno upādiyiṁ. Tassa me upādāna-paccayā bhavo, bhava-paccayā jāti, jāti-paccayā jarāmaraṇaṁ soka-parideva-dukkha-domanass-upāyāsā sambhavanti; evametassa kevalassa dukkhak-khandhassa samudayo hotī. (MN75)
--------------------
**Kāmaloka:** Upaniyyati ((upaneti: bestow) swept away) loko addhuvo (Unstable). Atāṇo (tāṇa shelter) loko anabhissaro (issara manage). Assako (saka: own) loko, sabbaṁ pahāya (left) gamanīyan (gacchati > leave). Ūno (deficient) loko atitto (titto fullfilled) taṇhādāso (Insatiable). (MN 82)
+ Mamāyite (being attached) passatha (side) phandamāne (trembling); Maccheva (fish) appodake (appa : little +udaka :water) khīṇa-sote (khīṇa : exhausted-khīyati+sota : a stream/torrent). (Snp 4.2)
+ Ajjhattikāni (interal) Saḷ-āyatana, bāhirāni (external) Saḷ-āyatanāni, 6 viññāna, 6 phassa, 6 vedana lujjati (fall apart), suññaṁ (empty) attena (self) vā attaniyena (belong to self), tasmā lokoti vuccatī, tasmā suñño lokoti vuccatī (Sn35.82) (Sn35.85).
--------------------
**Kamma**: Ekacco puggalo abhāvita-kāyo hoti abhāvita-sīlo abhāvita-citto abhāvita-pañño paritto (small) appa-ātuma (appa: small + ātuma = atta) appa-dukkha-vihārī. Evarūpassa, appamattakampi pāpakammaṁ kataṁ tamenaṁ nirayaṁ upaneti. (An3.100)
+ Dandhañhi (slow) karoto puññaṁ (merit), pāpasmiṁ ramatī (delighted in) mano... Ma-avamaññetha (disregard) pāpassa, na mantaṁ āgamissati (come back); Udabindu (water drop)-nipātena (nipāta: falling), udakumbhopi (water jar) pūrati (make full); Bālo (fool) pūrati pāpassa, thokaṁ thokampi (bit by bit) ācinaṁ (accumulates). Māvamaññetha puññassa, na mandaṁ āgamissati; Udabindunipātena, udakumbhopi pūrati; Dhīro(wise) pūrati puññassa, thokaṁ thokampi ācinaṁ. (Dhp116)
+ Pañcimāni (5) byasanāni (losses) / sampadā (gain): Ñāti (family), bhoga (wealth), roga (health), sīla, diṭṭhi. Na sattā ñāti/bhoga/roga, eva sīla/diṭṭhi-byasanahetu/sampadāhetu vā kāyassa bhedā paraṁ maraṇā [apāyaṁ duggatiṁ vinipātaṁ nirayaṁ]/[sugatiṁ saggaṁ lokaṁ] upapajjanti. (An5.130)
+ Lābho alābho ca yasāyaso ca, Nindā pasaṁsā ca sukhaṁ dukhañca; Ete aniccā manujesu (manuja : human being) dhammā, Asassatā (an:not- sastasa:eternal) vipariṇāma(change)-dhammā. (An8.6)
--------------------
**Nīvaraṇā:** yathā iṇaṁ (debt) yathā rogaṁ (disease) yathā bandhana-agāraṁ (prison) yathā dāsabyaṁ (slavery) yathā kantāra (wilderness)-addhāna (long journey)-maggaṁ, evaṁ ime pañca nīvaraṇe appahīne (not removed) attani samanupassati. Seyyathāpi yathā āṇaṇyaṁ yathā ārogyaṁ yathā bandhana-mokkhaṁ yathā bhujissaṁ (free man) yathā khema (safe)-anta (destination)-bhūmiṁ (place); Evameva kho ime pañca nīvaraṇe pahīne attani samanupassati. (DN 2)
Yasmiṁ samaye (whenever) kāma-rāga-pariyuṭṭhitena (pervaded) cetasā viharati kāma-rāga-paretena (overcomed), uppannassa ca kāmarāgassa nissaraṇaṁ (escape from arisen kamaraga) yathābhūtaṁ (truly) nap-pajānāti. attatthampi-paratthampi-ubhayatthampi (benefit for self, other, both) tasmiṁ samaye yathābhūtaṁ na jānāti na passati. Seyyathāpi udapatto saṁsaṭṭho lākhāya vā haliddiyā vā nīliyā vā mañjiṭṭhāya vā.
+ Byāpāda: uda-patto agginā santatto (burn) pakkuthito (boiled) usmā (heat)-udaka (water)-jāto. (bubbling)
+ Thīna-middha: udapatto sevāla (moss)-paṇaka (leaf)-pariyonaddho (overgrown, cover up).
+ Uddhacca-kukkucca: udapatto vāterito (vāta:wind + īrita:moved) calito (stirred) bhanto (whirling) ūmijāto (rippled).
+ Vicikiccha: udapatto āvilo luḷito kalalībhūto andhakāre nikkhitto. (Sn46.55)
--------------------
#### Bhaya (Danger)
+ Sabbaṁ (All: 6 Body faculty, its vinnana, its contact, Senses input & Vedana made : sukhaṁ vā dukkhaṁ vā adukkhamasukhaṁ vā) ādittaṁ. Rāgagginā, dosagginā, mohagginā ādittaṁ, jātiyā (birth) jarāya maraṇena sokehi (grief/sorrow) paridevehi (lamentation) dukkhehi (pain) domanassehi (despair) upāyāsehi (sadness) ādittaṁ. Evaṁ passaṁ, sabbaṁ nibbindati (fed-up/dissatisfied). Nibbindaṁ virajjati (detach); virāgā vimuccati (released); vimuttasmiṁ vimuttamiti ñāṇaṁ hoti (when freed, they know they free). (sn35.28)
+ Maccho (fish) gilita(swallowed)-baḷiso(hook) bāḷisikassa(hook-maker) anayaṁ (loss) āpanno (entered) byasanaṁ (ruin) āpanno yathā-kāmakaraṇīyo (do as wish) bāḷisikassa. Evameva kho, chayime (six) baḷisā lokasmiṁ anayāya sattānaṁ (satta:living being) vadhāya (killing) pāṇinaṁ (Pāṇin:pāṇa:living being), bhikkhu gilitabaḷiso mārassa anayaṁ āpanno byasanaṁ āpanno yathākāmakaraṇīyo pāpimato (Mara:WickedOne).  (sn35.230)
+ Perplexed mind: Indriya-nimitta (features of sense input) anusari (anusarati:follow) viññāṇaṁ hoti, nimitt-assāda-gadhitaṁ (assāda: enjoy - gadhita: tied) nimitt-assāda-vinibandhaṁ (vinibandha: attached/bondage) nimitt-assāda-saṁyojana-saṁyuttaṁ (saṁyojana: fetter - saṁyutta: connected/combined) bahiddhā (externally) viññāṇaṁ vikkhittaṁ (perplexed) visaṭanti (diffused). Na indriya-nimittānusāri viññāṇaṁ hoti, na nimittassādagadhitaṁ na nimitt-assāda-vinibandhaṁ na nimitt-assāda-saṁyojana-saṁyuttaṁ bahiddhā  viññāṇaṁ avikkhittaṁ avisaṭanti. (MN138)
