#### Dāna-maha-pphala
1. Dāna as investment, with mind attached to it, thinking "I'll reap reward next life".
2. Dāna as traditions, thinking "This is not right for me to abandon this tradition".
3. Dāna as a good quality, thinking "Giving is good".
4. Dāna as karuna, thinking "I can cook, they can't. It is not right not to give".
5. Dāna as sacrify, thinking "There were great sacrifices of past sages, in the same way, this is my sacrifices".
6. Dāna as joy, thinking "Giving make the mind serene with joy and gratification".
7. Dāna as support for the mind, thinking "This is an ornament for the mind, a support for the mind". This is the greatest fruit of Dāna

+ Buddha: "If sentient beings only knew, as I do, the fruit of giving and sharing, they would not eat without first giving, and the stain of stinginess would not occupy their minds. They would not eat without sharing even their last mouthful, their last morsel, so long as there was someone to receive it. It is because sentient beings do not know, as I do, the fruit of giving and sharing, that they eat without first giving, and the stain of stinginess occupies their minds.” (Iti 26)
