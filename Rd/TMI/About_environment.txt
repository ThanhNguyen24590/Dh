#### Time:
* Make meditation a priority, make time (not spare time). Have a schedule & fixed period to meditate. Never miss a daily practice session except when absolutely unavoidable
* When at least agitated or tired. Take mental energy & clarity to account.
* When not conflict with other activities/ obligations.
* Minimum: 45 minutes
#### Space
* Have a regular space, specific for meditation.
* Design/ have stuff that inspire/remind of why to meditate: altar, books....
##### Body Posture
* Little physical strain or pain as possible. Eliminate discomfort that you can, but accept what remains as part of practice.
* Don’t stop meditating when change position:  move slowly\deliberately with full attention to the sensations in body as you shift.
##### Attitude
* Practice in a relaxed manner, free from judgment and expectations.
* Generate an attitude of faith, trust, and confidence: faith in the method, trust that the results will come with continued practice, and confidence in your own ability.
