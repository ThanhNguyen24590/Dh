=== 5 indriya: ===
**Vitakka**: make med obj notifiable, captivated. Do not overthink, 
+ Light, Clarity & Purity: resolution & details vs confused, ignored, blurry. Viz: Lighthouse
+ Enlarge: zoom in | obj grow | stretch-widen obj | Viz big/infinite scene/obj: sky, ocean,....
+ Beautify: regc the rythm (not mechanical) | Viz beautiful scene/obj: nature
	+ Repulsify: reveal that repulsed obj as part of normality > detachment
+ Divine/Mystify/Sanctify: view obj as alive, powerful
**Vicara**: change to be with obj long time
+ Safety: not critize, let go. Like: House for stray animal. Some'll leave, some'll stay. Just keep the safety space .
+ Comfort: obj should for body & mind. Even with repulsive, comfort by detachment to the obj.
+ Stimulate: obj should bring refresh, & joy, drilling more and more to your deep emotion/being/
+ Refine: gradually. Like pond muddied by passenger. Don't disturb it more, just let it settle down by itself.
