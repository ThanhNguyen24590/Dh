=== Dhammadāyādā: tena na ādiyo bhaveyyaṁ - āmisadāyādā: tena ādiyo bhaveyyaṁ ===
+ Dhammadāyādā me, bhikkhave, bhavatha, mā āmisadāyādā. Atthi me tumhesu anukampā: ‘kinti me sāvakā dhammadāyādā bhaveyyuṁ, no āmisadāyādā’”ti.
+ .....Tañhi tassa, bhikkhave, bhikkhuno dīgharattaṁ appicchatāya santuṭṭhiyā sallekhāya subharatāya vīriyārambhāya saṁvattissati.
**Lineage: Pháp tử, Phật tử.**
Duty to upheld reputation/honor of Lineage-Tradition, or in Buddhism as a whole?
This contribute to the practice, unsuprisingly, appicchatāya santuṭṭhiyā sallekhāya subharatāya, supprisingly, vīriyārambhāya.

=== Vivekaṁ nānusikkhanti: gārayha - Vivekamanusikkhantī: pāsaṁsa ===
+ Kittāvatā nu kho, āvuso, satthu pavivittassa viharato sāvakā vivekaṁ nānusikkhanti? Idhāvuso, satthu pavivittassa viharato sāvakā vivekaṁ nānusikkhanti, yesañca dhammānaṁ satthā pahānamāha, te ca dhamme nappajahanti, bāhulikā ca honti, sāthalikā, okkamane pubbaṅgamā, **paviveke nikkhittadhurā**. Tatrāvuso, [therā-majjhimā-navā] bhikkhū tīhi ṭhānehi gārayhā bhavanti.
+ Kittāvatā ca panāvuso, satthu pavivittassa viharato sāvakā vivekamanusikkhanti? Idhāvuso, satthu pavivittassa viharato sāvakā vivekamanusikkhanti—yesañca dhammānaṁ satthā pahānamāha te ca dhamme pajahanti; na ca bāhulikā honti, na sāthalikā okkamane nikkhittadhurā paviveke pubbaṅgamā. Tatrāvuso, [therā-majjhimā-navā] bhikkhū tīhi ṭhānehi pāsaṁsā bhavanti.
=== Majjhimā paṭipadā ===
Katamā ca sā, āvuso, majjhimā paṭipadā cakkhukaraṇī ñāṇakaraṇī upasamāya abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṁvattati? Ayameva ariyo aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo, seyyathidaṁ—sammādiṭṭhi sammāsaṅkappo sammāvācā sammākammanto sammāājīvo sammāvāyāmo sammāsati sammāsamādhi.
