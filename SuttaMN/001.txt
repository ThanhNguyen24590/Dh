=== Settings: Visualize ===
Evaṁ me sutaṁ—ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā ukkaṭṭhāyaṁ viharati subhagavane sālarājamūle.
Ukkaṭṭhā: primary center of Kosalan Brahmanism, brahmadeya of leading brahmin Pokkharasāti. In country Kosalan, near Icchānaṅgala, not far from Sāvatthī to the east.
+ People involved: 
	. Teacher: Buddha: dispel the monks misconception of immortal self. Use style: formal lecture.
	. Listerner: Monk previously being Brahma. How do they feel from moment to moment?
	. Yourself as audience:
=== Diagram ===
+ Thirst/craving (Taṇhā) > Grasping (Upādāna) : abhinandati > maññati
+ 1 triangle: Loka: Kama-Rupa-Arupa + 1 triangle: Sila-Samadhi-Panna > Hexagon
+ 1 dot in hexagon: symbol bound by 3 worlds: assutavā puthujjano
+ 1 circle in hexagon: represent sekkha, not out of bound, but with strength & potential (by the 3 training) to break out.
+ Dot outside hexagon, at point: 4 dot for Arahat + 2 dot for Buddha.

=== Kama Loka: Immaterial World ===

Yopi so, bhikkhave, bhikkhu arahaṁ khīṇāsavo vusitavā katakaraṇīyo ohitabhāro anuppattasadattho parikkhīṇabhavasaṁyojano sammadaññāvimutto, sopi pathaviṁ pathavito abhijānāti; pathaviṁ pathavito abhiññāya pathaviṁ na maññati, pathaviyā na maññati, pathavito na maññati, pathaviṁ meti na maññati, pathaviṁ nābhinandati.
